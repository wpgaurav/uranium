# Credits and provenance

## Images in theme/assets/images

Each image was created for Uranium on 2026-09-12 with xAI Grok Imagine, through the gpt-image skill's Grok route. That route crops the generator's corner wordmark from every frame. Codex, the skill's default engine, timed out on every run that day, so Grok was used for the whole set.

The files were converted to WebP with Pillow, which writes no EXIF, XMP or C2PA data. The product photos had their off-white backgrounds lifted to pure white, so they blend into Uranium's product plates. `product-pump-cutout.webp` is the pump with its background removed, for the dark spec strip.

No image was copied from, traced from or prompted with any existing website or photograph. Released under CC0 1.0.
